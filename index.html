# Repurpose Studio Privacy Policy **Last updated:** October 11, 2026 Repurpose Studio is a college project that helps users transform long-form content into social media drafts and schedule posts. ## Information We Handle Depending on the features you use, Repurpose Studio may process article text, generated drafts, scheduled publication dates, and LinkedIn profile information needed to connect your account. ## LinkedIn Account and Publishing If you connect LinkedIn, the application uses LinkedIn's authorization process to access the permissions you approve. It may store access tokens and profile information to support the connection and publishing features. We use LinkedIn data only to provide the application's features. We do not sell personal information. ## Storage and Security Article content, drafts, and scheduling information may be stored in a MongoDB database. Access tokens and other credentials must be protected and must not be published in source code or public repositories. No internet-based application can guarantee absolute security. ## Data Retention and Deletion Information may remain stored until it is deleted from the application or its database. Users who need assistance with deletion can contact the project maintainer. ## Third-Party Services The application may use LinkedIn for authentication and publishing and MongoDB Atlas for data storage. Those services process data according to their own terms and privacy policies. ## Contact For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the project maintainer through the GitHub repository where this policy is published.
